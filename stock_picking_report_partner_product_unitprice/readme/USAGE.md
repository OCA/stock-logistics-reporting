To use this module, you need to:

1. Go to **Contacts** and open a partner.
2. In the **Sales & Purchase** tab, locate the **Inventory** section and enable **Render Unit Price on Delivery Slip**.
3. Create or open a delivery order for this partner.
4. Print the **Delivery Slip** report.

When enabled, the unit price from the related sales order line is displayed on the delivery slip. If the picking
contains products with different unit prices, they are displayed as separate aggregated lines. Products with the s
ame unit price can still be aggregated.

The unit price column is only displayed when the setting is enabled and at least one delivery line has a
unit price from a related sales order line.
