To configure this module, you need to:

1.  Go to *Inventory ▸ Configuration ▸ Settings*, section *Undelivered
    Products*, and select the method in "Undelivered Product Lines in
    Delivery Slip Report":
    - *Display all undelivered product lines* (default).
    - *Display only partially undelivered product lines*.
    - *Display only completely undelivered product lines*.
2.  On the customer form (*Contacts* app), tab *Sales & Purchase*, group
    *Misc*, check "Display Undelivered In Picking". It is checked by
    default.
3.  On the product form (*Inventory ▸ Products ▸ Products*), tab
    *Inventory*, section *Logistics* (goods products only), check
    "Display Undelivered In Picking". It is checked by default.
