To use this module, you need to:

1. Activate the developer mode
2. Go to **Contacts** and open a partner.
3. In the **Sales & Purchase**-tab look for **Inventory** section and enable **Render Barcode on Delivery Slip**.
4. Create or open a delivery order for this partner.
5. Print the **Delivery Slip** report.

The product barcode column is displayed when the setting is enabled and at least one
product on the picking has a barcode.
