This module adds an optional product barcode to the delivery slip report.
The barcode display can be configured per partner through the Render Barcode on Delivery Slip setting in the
partner's Inventory section in Sales & Purchase Tab.

When enabled, product barcodes are displayed on the delivery slip if at least one product
on the picking has a barcode. If no product barcode is available, the barcode column is not displayed.

The setting is company-dependent to support different configurations for the same partner across multiple companies.
