This module adds a **Signed Value** to stock moves, in the company currency.
It is the move value, positive when the goods enter the company stock and
negative when they leave it. Moves that stay inside the company stock, like
internal transfers, have a signed value of zero.

The signed value is shown in the *Moves Analysis* list with its total, and it
is available as a measure in the pivot view, to analyse the net value of the
stock moves.
